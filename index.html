Sundanese96 AutoPoster Studio

A private desktop tool for uploading and scheduling videos and Shorts on YouTube channels the operator owns or manages.

How the app works

  1. Prepare a video. The operator selects a content folder on their computer with a finished video, metadata and an optional thumbnail.
  2. Authorize a channel. The desktop app opens Google’s consent screen to connect to the channel the operator manages. This homepage is publicly readable.
  3. Upload or schedule. The app sends the video and its settings to YouTube using the YouTube Data API. The operator selects the channel, content, visibility and any scheduled release time.
  4. Check performance. The operator can request channel and video statistics, including views, watch time and audience retention, through the desktop app.

Why the app requests access

The app uses Google authorization to identify the connected YouTube channel, upload the operator’s videos and thumbnails, and retrieve performance data for that channel. The Privacy Policy explains the data the app accesses, where it is stored and how to revoke access.

Permitted use

The app uploads media prepared by the operator. Use is limited to content the operator has the rights and permissions to publish. The Terms of Service prohibit using the app or its Google API access to create, upload or distribute non-consensual intimate imagery, including AI-generated imagery.
