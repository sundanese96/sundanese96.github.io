Sundanese96 AutoPoster Studio

A Windows desktop app by Sundanese96 for uploading and scheduling videos and Shorts on YouTube and reviewing channel performance.

Read the Privacy Policy

What the app does

Sundanese96 AutoPoster Studio helps the operator publish finished videos to YouTube channels they own or manage. The operator selects a content folder, destination channel, video metadata, optional thumbnail, and visibility or release time. The desktop app can preview the metadata and then send the selected files and settings to YouTube.

The operator can also check recent uploads and see channel and video statistics such as views, watch time, and audience retention from the desktop app.

Why the app requests Google access

  • Connect the selected channel. Google OAuth 2.0 lets the desktop app identify and act on the YouTube channel the operator authorizes.
  • Publish videos. The YouTube Data API receives the selected video, thumbnail, and publishing settings and returns the video ID.
  • Show channel performance. The YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs provide statistics and reports for the authorized channel.

Anyone can read this website without signing in. The desktop app opens Google’s consent screen when the operator connects a channel. The Privacy Policy explains which data the desktop app uses, how it stores that data, and how to revoke access.

Permitted use

The app sends finished files supplied by the operator. It has no image or video generation feature. The operator must have the rights and permissions to publish those files.

The Terms of Service prohibit using the app or its Google API access to create, upload, or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII) or any other non-consensual intimate imagery. Use of the APIs is subject to the Google APIs Terms of Service.
